#01 VERIFICATION PREREQUISITES & HARDWARE PERMISSIONS

5-Point Verification Checklist

Ensure each prerequisite is satisfied to enable autonomous background synchronization between iOS and your Mac.

STEP 01 IDENTITY
Identical Apple ID
Your iPhone and your Mac must be signed into the same Apple ID. The app writes into the private ubiquity container iCloud~com~hermes~HermesCompanion which only synchronizes between devices sharing your account.
STEP 02 STORAGE
iCloud Drive Enabled
On iPhone: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → iCloud Drive must be toggled ON.
On macOS: System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → iCloud Drive must also be enabled.
STEP 03 CRITICAL
Location Permission: "Always"
In iOS Settings, location access must be set to "Always" (not just "While Using the App"). iOS suspends background wakes, cell-tower monitoring, and stationary geofences if set only to "While Using".
STEP 04 HEALTH & MOTION
Apple Health Authorization
When prompted by the app, authorize read access for Sleep Analysis and Workouts, plus Motion & Fitness. Without this, health snapshots will remain empty.
FIG. SUP-01 VERIFIED PROFILE
Target Settings State
Verified configuration: Smart Always profile active, dynamic stationary geofence set to 100m, and distance filter set to 30m.
